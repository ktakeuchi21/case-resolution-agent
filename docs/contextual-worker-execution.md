# Contextual composition implementation

The deployed baseline is `acaabee` (runtime `e1528b7`, synthesis prompt v5). It interprets ordinary conversation with rules and templates work products after generating claims. The new pipeline must give the configured model responsibility for interpretation and complete composition while retaining deterministic retrieval eligibility, exact citations, workflow authority and durable isolation.

## Sequence and verification

1. Freeze a paired A–M conversation catalog and capture the current live baseline before changing deployment. Preserve failed checks and all earlier artifacts.
2. Add a versioned interpretation and complete-turn contract. Supply bounded, typed memory and the active artifact. Retrieve fresh evidence after interpretation. Validate the complete output, exact quotes, current eligibility, authority, channel restrictions and requested transformations; independently review factual coverage.
3. Keep recipient copy separate from server-owned operator context. Clarify only material ambiguity with a recorded decision. Represent evidence identification and recorded follow-up stages consistently without granting chat execution capabilities.
4. Add durable bounded regeneration, feedback and validated stage progress. Compact older turns, retain accessible composer and collapsed evidence, and polish knowledge coverage labels.
5. Add unit, PostgreSQL/application, workflow and frozen conversation checks. Run the full existing regression suite. Inspect desktop, tablet and mobile browsers, including all A–M traces and failure/retry behavior.
6. Deploy to the existing Render service, rerun the frozen live conversations, inspect exact evidence and copy, retain measured latency/usage and explain limitations. Publish architecture, product, evaluation, decision and developer documentation with a final verification report.

No model change is planned: retain `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14`. All integrations remain synthetic. Requests consume the existing durable provider budget, including failures; no automatic retry or fallback. Offline provider fixtures prove contracts, not live quality. Natural-language entailment review remains fallible; exact quote and authority checks remain deterministic.

The workflow engine remains the sole transition owner. Evidence can identify a missing document before follow-up is prepared. The case presentation must distinguish that finding from recorded preparation, authorization and dispatch, preserving the exact payer-decision boundary.

## Acceptance map

A/B: reference resolution and direct rationale/evidence. C–F: complete recipient copy, exact artifact lineage, ≥25% reduction and restricted SMS. G: material recipient ambiguity and clarification rationale. H: explicitly unverified reported communication. I/J: context switching and retirement with unchanged history. K: intake, evidence identification and follow-up preparation. L: payer, clinical and real-send boundaries. M: at least eight mixed turns at compact width, persistent composer and expandable evidence. Regeneration must perform fresh retrieval and validation within a durable cap; feedback must remain scoped and must not alter historical answers.
