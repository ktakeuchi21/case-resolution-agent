Downloaded October 9, 2026 under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Pexels permits free use and modification; it prohibits implying endorsement by the people pictured. These are stock photos, not generated portraits or photographs of actual Pathway personnel.

The images are served locally as small WebP files, 256 pixels wide. Documentation and Access use square crops supplied by the image CDN; Fulfillment keeps its portrait aspect ratio and uses CSS framing. No image-service request is made by visitors. Explicit display dimensions reserve space, scenario-card portraits load lazily, and a Pathway lettermark remains if an image fails. Historical answers select the portrait by their own recorded Knowledge Pack, not the conversation's current pack. No case, retrieval, generation, model, or rate-limit settings change.
